- 新增收集箱，包含 **Work 1 / Work 2 / Life** 三组，沿用看板的字体与任务样式。
  Add an inbox with **Work 1 / Work 2 / Life**, matching the board's typography and task styles.

  Use **Schedule for** to move an inbox task to a day in the displayed week, or **Move to inbox** to move a board task into a group.
- 收集箱跨周保留，支持撤销，与周计划一起本地保存。
  Keep inbox tasks across weeks, with undo and local saving alongside weekly plans.
